### ALGO-000040: VP versus VNP
- **Subfield:** Algebraic complexity and polynomial identity testing
- **Open:** Determine whether every polynomial family in VNP has polynomial-size arithmetic circuits; over characteristic zero, separating the permanent from VP would suffice.
- **Status:** Valiant introduced the classes in 1979. Restricted circuit lower bounds are extensive, but the general separation remains open.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** This is the central algebraic analogue of efficient computation versus efficient exponential summation.
- **Key works:** Valiant (1979), *Completeness Classes in Algebra*; Shpilka and Yehudayoff (2010), *Arithmetic Circuits: A Survey of Recent Results and Open Questions*, DOI: 10.1561/0400000039.
- **Related:** Algebraic geometry—explicit equations distinguishing complexity classes; statistical physics—partition-function complexity.
